**Lisyn (the “App”) does not collect any personally identifiable information from users who install the App.**

The App requires access to the device's Bluetooth module to connect with other devices, sync musical playlists, and manage voting information for songs. Additionally, the App requires access to your music and media play songs from the Apple Music library.

The App (version 1.3.0 and above) collects the following data for the purposes of App usage analytics:
- Anonymized device-level identifiers (reset upon App reinstallation)
- Anonymized usage data (e.g., app launches, screens viewed, features used)
- Anonymized crash logs

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/lisyn_app).
